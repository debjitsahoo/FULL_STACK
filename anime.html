The Big Three of Anime

The Big Three refers to the three most influential shonen anime that dominated the 2000s and introduced millions of fans around the world to anime. They are Naruto, One Piece, and Bleach.

1. Naruto

Creator: Masashi Kishimoto

Genre: Action, Adventure, Fantasy

Naruto follows the journey of Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the strongest and most respected leader of the Hidden Leaf Village. Along the way, he forms friendships, faces powerful enemies, and never gives up on his dreams.

Main Characters

  • Naruto Uzumaki
  • Sasuke Uchiha
  • Sakura Haruno
  • Kakashi Hatake

2. One Piece

Creator: Eiichiro Oda

Genre: Adventure, Action, Comedy

One Piece tells the story of Monkey D. Luffy, a pirate with a dream of finding the legendary treasure known as the One Piece and becoming the Pirate King. Along with his Straw Hat Pirates, he travels across the Grand Line facing powerful foes and making lifelong friends.

Main Characters

  • Monkey D. Luffy
  • Roronoa Zoro
  • Nami
  • Sanji
  • Tony Tony Chopper

3. Bleach

Creator: Tite Kubo

Genre: Action, Supernatural

Bleach follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper. His mission is to protect humans from evil spirits called Hollows and maintain the balance between the human world and the Soul Society.

Main Characters

  • Ichigo Kurosaki
  • Rukia Kuchiki
  • Orihime Inoue
  • Uryu Ishida
  • Yasutora "Chad" Sado

Dragon Ball – The Legend That Inspired the Big Three

Creator: Akira Toriyama

Genre: Action, Adventure, Martial Arts, Fantasy

Dragon Ball is not officially part of the Big Three because it was released much earlier. However, it is widely regarded as the anime that inspired the Big Three. Its influence on modern shonen anime is enormous, and many creators have cited Akira Toriyama as a major inspiration.

The story follows Son Goku, a Saiyan warrior who trains tirelessly to become stronger and protects Earth from powerful enemies. Dragon Ball introduced iconic transformations such as the Super Saiyan and helped define the modern battle anime genre.

Main Characters

  • Son Goku
  • Vegeta
  • Gohan
  • Piccolo
  • Bulma

Why Dragon Ball is Special

  • Inspired the creators of Naruto, One Piece, and Bleach.
  • Popularized modern shonen battle anime.
  • Introduced the legendary Super Saiyan transformation.
  • One of the best-selling manga series of all time.
  • Influenced generations of anime fans around the world.
